01 / Our focus

Four areas of work.

From the mechanism to device motion and feedback tests.

  1. 01

    Mechanics

    Segmented structures, tendon routing, and bending and rotation mechanisms.

  2. 02

    Embedded control

    Firmware and communication for motor and servo control.

  3. 03

    Software & integration

    Command tools, data formats and repeatable integration tests.

  4. 04

    Assembly & testing

    Assembly, calibration, and tests of device motion and feedback.
